How it works

Your escrow payment went up. Is the math right?

Escrow is the part of your mortgage payment that your servicer (the company you send the payment to) sets aside to pay your property taxes and insurance. A federal rule limits how much they may collect, and once a year they must send you a statement showing their math.

  1. 01

    Type the numbers

    Copy a few numbers from your yearly escrow statement into four short steps. A sample statement shows where to find each one.

  2. 02

    The federal math runs on your device

    Add up the year’s bills, divide by 12, walk the balance forward month by month, and keep a cushion no bigger than one sixth of the year’s bills. That is the method in federal rule 12 CFR 1024.17.

  3. 03

    A plain answer and a letter

    A verdict in plain English, every step of the math, and a calm letter you can send your servicer if a number does not add up.
